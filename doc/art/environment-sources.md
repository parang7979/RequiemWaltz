# 종탑과 석교의 화면 기준 원화

2026-11-18에 내장 ImageGen으로 생성한 원화 두 장이다. 입력 참조 이미지는 사용하지 않았다. 원화는 [환경 제작 폴더](../../artsource/environment)에 보존한다. 같은 입력 그림에서 게임 텍스처를 다시 만들 때는 저장소 루트에서 `python artsource/environment/pixelize.py`를 실행한다. 기준 버전은 Python 3.10과 Pillow 9.4.0이다.

원경은 가로 704픽셀·56색으로 줄여 카메라에 패럴랙스 여백을 준다. 발판은 960픽셀·48색으로 줄이고 알파 경계를 정리한다. 충돌 기준은 난간 아래 돌 상판이다. `causeway_normal.png`는 불투명 석재의 명도 기울기로 근사한 얕은 요철이다. 문양이나 재질별 깊이는 정식 타일 제작 때 다시 잡는다.

## 원경 생성 프롬프트

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: single widescreen background matte for a side-scrolling gothic pixel-art action game. A huge ruined bell abbey on a distant cliff on the right half, a stack of Black Forest hills and a sunken town on the lower left, a pale crescent moon in a band of pale blue clouds in the upper left. Evening after rain, smoky desaturated blue-violet shadows, bronze and faded rose in the cloud edges, a handful of amber-lit high arched windows. Moody, exquisite hand-painted theatrical atmosphere, elegantly simplified shape design, clearly separated, delicately textured color masses that can be reduced to a 640x360 pixel palette. Side elevation over the valley, distant landscape only, broad horizontal framing, upper half interesting sky, silhouetted architecture center-right at middle height. The lower quarter is very hazy quiet planes; it will be hidden by a separately built playable bridge. Full bleed 16:9, no border, no text, no UI, no foreground platforms or objects, no people, no lettering or logo. Original architecture, haunting but readable, let soft light pool behind the forms.

## 석교 생성 프롬프트

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: isolated, horizontally oriented 2D sidescroller environment layer on genuine transparent background. Original abandoned stone abbey causeway viewed purely in flat side elevation, a continuous horizontal playable walkway crossing fully from the left image edge to the right image edge with NO gaps. Top surface of stone path a perfectly straight horizontal line across whole composition. Below this line a roughly 2-foot thick coping of flat, uneven slate voussoirs, then a continuous row of wide elegant pointed gothic arch openings through which fully transparent background is visible, with thick damp stone piers suggesting a deep valley beneath. Above the walkway sparse thin low decorative wrought iron railings with breaks, a few tufts of dried grass, no high wall. Flat game kit elevation, long panoramic composition 3:1, building fills width, minimal empty margin above and below. Dark ink blue and dusky muted mauve stone, pale fine desaturated light catching the very top edge; weathered small masonry and creeping ivy with refined purple shadows. Crisp stylized hand-painted blockwork, simplified clusters ready for pixelation. Transparent around bridge including inside open arches; no background, no sky, no ground plane, no approach road leading into depth, no perspective or vanishing point, no figures, no lights, no text.
